
## Problem/Solution
❌ Problem Statement

**In web3, achievements aren’t truly owned or verifiable.** When someone completes a course, contributes to a project, attends an event, or reaches a milestone, the “proof” usually lives off‑chain — Discord roles, screenshots, centralized badges, or Google Forms. These can be faked, lost, or revoked, and they don’t integrate with the broader ecosystem.

There’s no trustless, portable, on‑chain way for users to prove what they’ve accomplished.

This creates three major issues:

 - No verifiable identity layer for skills, participation, or contributions
 - No standard format for achievements across communities
 - No ownership — users can’t carry their accomplishments between platforms or dApps
 - In a space built on transparency and decentralization, achievement systems are still stuck in Web2.

✅ Solution Statement
**My project solves this by creating an on‑chain achievement badge system using ERC‑1155 tokens.**
Users can mint verifiable badges directly on an EVM chain, each representing a completed task, milestone, or contribution. These badges are stored in the user’s wallet, making them portable, trustless, and permanently verifiable.

Key elements of the solution:

 - ERC‑1155 smart contract for flexible, gas‑efficient badge types
 - Signature‑based claims (EIP‑712) for secure, permissioned badge distribution
 - Optional soulbound logic to prevent transfers and preserve authenticity
 - Front‑end interface where users connect their wallet and claim badges
 - On‑chain metadata that describes the achievement and timestamp
